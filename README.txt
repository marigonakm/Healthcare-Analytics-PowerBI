Healthcare Analytics & Revenue Cycle Performance | Power BI

Project Overview

An end-to-end healthcare analytics project developed in Microsoft Power BI to evaluate clinical operations, patient demand, hospital performance, and revenue cycle management.

The project brings together clinical and financial performance indicators to identify operational inefficiencies, understand patient utilization patterns, assess revenue collection performance, and highlight financial risks associated with insurance claims and denials.

Through interactive dashboards and key performance indicators (KPIs), the analysis supports data-driven decision-making across healthcare facilities and departments.

Business Objectives

1. Clinical Operations & Patient Demand

Analyze how patient demand has evolved over time across healthcare facilities, departments, and service lines.

Key questions:
- Which facility is experiencing the fastest year-over-year growth in patient volume?
- How has the distribution of Inpatient, Outpatient, and Emergency Room encounters changed over the last four years?
- Are there seasonal patterns or recurring spikes in patient demand?

Key Performance Indicators:
- Total Encounters
- Average Length of Stay (LOS)
- Total Readmissions
- Readmission Rate (%)

2. Operational Efficiency & Patient Outcomes

Identify operational pressure points by examining patient volume, severity, length of stay, readmissions, and patient satisfaction.

Key questions:
- Which departments consume the most hospital bed capacity based on patient volume and length of stay?
- Is patient severity associated with higher readmission rates?
- Which departments combine low patient satisfaction scores with high readmission rates?

3. Financial Performance & Revenue Analysis

Evaluate financial performance by comparing billed, allowed, and paid amounts to understand revenue realization and collection efficiency.

Key questions:
- What percentage of gross billed revenue is reduced through insurance contractual adjustments?
- Which facilities demonstrate the highest net collection rates, and which are underperforming?
- Are cash collections keeping pace with patient service volumes?

Key Performance Indicators:
- Total Claims
- Total Billed
- Total Allowed
- Total Paid
- Total Write-Off Amount
- Total Overpaid
- Collection Rate (%)

4. Revenue Cycle Management & Financial Risk

Identify revenue cycle vulnerabilities related to insurance denials, payment delays, and uncollectible revenue.

Key questions:
- What are the most frequent reasons for insurance claim denials?
- Which insurance payers present the highest financial risk based on denial rates and payment delays?
- How much revenue is written off as uncollectible due to unresolved or expired claim denials?


*Note: Negative Patient Responsibility amounts are treated as overpayments in this analysis. Percentage measures are expressed as percentages.*

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI – Interactive dashboards and data visualization
- Power Query – Data transformation and preparation
- DAX – Calculated measures and KPIs
- Data Analytics – Exploratory analysis, trend analysis, and performance monitoring
